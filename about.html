About

This is a website...

Richard Charles Boxley (aka Wave Tenet) is a composer, educator, and researcher working with modular synthesis, field recording, and experimental music. He teaches music at Kidderminster College and is currently undertaking a PhD in composition at the University of Birmingham, exploring creative constraints and modular synthesis. Richard also runs a growing YouTube channel where he documents his creative process, shares insights into modular systems, and discuss creativity and the creative process. His work has been featured in the Cities and Memory global sound project and has also been included in the British Library Sound Archive’s Sound and Moving Image Collection.
